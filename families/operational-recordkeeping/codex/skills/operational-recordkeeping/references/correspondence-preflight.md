# Financial Correspondence Preflight

Apply before drafting/editing financial correspondence and again before reporting
it ready. This is an agent evidence-review procedure, not a semantic audit
performed by the helper, a new accounting system or permission to send anything.

## Records First

1. Read the actual incoming request, including requested documents and questions.
   Define the response's purpose: fulfil the request, give an explicitly partial
   response, or ask an intentional clarification. Do not change that purpose just
   because producing text is easier than resolving the evidence.
2. Locate the current ledger/register, correspondence tracker and evidence through
   the project's existing bindings. Inspect the relevant entries and linked files
   before claiming information is missing or asking the user to provide it again.
   Use bounded lookups by invoice, transaction, property and period, not a wholesale
   reread. Distinguish not inspected, inaccessible, not located in checked sources,
   contradictory and genuinely unresolved. Thread recollection is a locator, not
   proof that the current records say the same thing.
3. Keep a compact working map in the task's private working context or evidence
   note, not as a new bookkeeping state: `claim/request | source + precise locator | evidence state | draft
   treatment / next action`. Cover material amounts, currency, dates/period,
   payer/payee, payment status, allocation and every requested document. Reuse
   current verified evidence where applicable; do not manufacture new paperwork.
4. Resolve discrepancies from available records/source evidence. Preserve factual
   provenance: user-reported, ledger-recorded and independently verified are not
   interchangeable. Ask only about material gaps the available evidence cannot
   resolve. Do not edit records merely to make an unsupported draft appear true.

## Keep Facts Separate

| Fact | What must not be inferred from it alone |
| --- | --- |
| account identity | A known personal/business account does not prove funds moved, the beneficiary or completion. |
| contractual obligation | Lease/invoice terms establish agreed amounts and periods, not actual payment. |
| actual payment | Inspect transaction status, amount/currency, date and destination before asserting completion; do not assume recipient acknowledgement. |
| payment allocation | A lump transfer is not automatically all rent, or rent for the lease's entire period. Check its documented allocation, including any deposit/fees, without inventing a split. |
| requested receipts | A transfer confirmation, lease and recipient-issued receipt are different documents. Do not claim a substitute meets the request without an explicit acceptance/decision. |

Independent existing evidence may establish an amount or period without one
particular attachment. That does not make an explicitly requested document
optional. Track factual support and document-request fulfilment separately.
Do not imply deductibility, reimbursement approval or accounting acceptance from
payment alone. Preserve the distinction between personal funding and company use.

## Saved Draft Review

- Before calling a saved draft ready, read back its actual authored body and
  attachment list using Outlook, not just the proposed text or save result.
- Compare each material statement and requested item with the working map. Remove
  accidental placeholders, contradictory pending/paid claims, unsupported amounts,
  periods or "all provided" claims. Do not erase legitimate uncertainty.
- A missing requested receipt blocks claiming complete fulfilment, not every
  possible email. An intentional clarification or partial response can be ready
  for that purpose when its wording accurately identifies the unresolved item.
  Do not silently substitute that purpose for the user's requested outcome.
- Report `draft saved, not sent` separately from readiness. If not ready, name the
  exact evidence gap. A successful save/attachment operation and a reconciliation
  `check` do not independently verify the truth or completeness of the email.
- Keep private preflight notes out of the recipient-facing body unless necessary
  to its purpose. Reconcile affected correspondence/obligation records after the
  actual action, without marking a draft as sent or a request as answered early.

## Review Scenarios

- Known payer account plus lease: contractual terms may be supported; actual
  payment and allocation are not established by those facts alone.
- Transfer confirmed but allocation unclear: assert only supported transaction
  facts; resolve the allocation before claiming a rent period was settled.
- Receipts explicitly requested but only a lease/transfer located: retain the
  request as unresolved; do not relabel the missing receipt optional.
- Records already contain the needed proof: inspect and use it, rather than put
  an avoidable payment question in the draft or ask the user to repeat themselves.
- User requests a clarification about missing receipts: draft that question
  honestly; it can be ready as a clarification without pretending receipts exist.
